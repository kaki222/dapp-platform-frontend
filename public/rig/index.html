What is simplified
  1. Everything is synthetic. Trouble events (stuck pipe, mud pump failure, waiting on weather, forced trip) are drawn at random, and each well has its own trouble level. Costs are assumed: $150,000 per rig day plus $520 per foot.
  2. Rig states come from simple threshold rules on hookload, block position, rotary speed and pump pressure, plus hole depth. This is a simplification: real systems use more channels and the daily drilling report. The rules were written against the same simulator, so the high match rate is flattering.
  3. NPT here means unplanned time: stuck pipe, pump failure, waiting on weather and forced trips. A forced trip looks the same as a planned trip on these four signals, so the rules cannot see it. That is the main reason found NPT is lower than true NPT.
  4. Days per 1,000 ft run from spud to total depth. The plan is 1.90 days per 1,000 ft. Wells delivered versus plan counts the wells whose planned finish date has passed.
