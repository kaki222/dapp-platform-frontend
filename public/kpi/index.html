Method and assumptions

Each well: footage, days per 1,000 ft (1.8–3.0), NPT share (5–35 %) and cost per foot ($700–1,200) drawn uniformly; rig days = footage ÷ 1,000 × days per 1,000 ft, NPT days = rig days × NPT share, cost = cost per foot × footage. Test 1 uses 3–10 wells per campaign; Test 2 two fields X and Y of 2–6 wells each, lower cost per foot ranked better.

Error = (average − pooled) ÷ pooled × 100, the same formula for all three KPIs. For NPT the report states the gap in percentage points; the check table shows both.

All wells are randomly generated: the results show how the roll-up method behaves, not real field performance. The ranges are assumptions chosen to bracket the report's examples.
